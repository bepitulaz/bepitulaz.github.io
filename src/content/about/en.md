Asep Bagja Priandana is a self-taught software engineer from Indonesia, based in Tallinn, Estonia. He started his professional career in 2010, worked for companies in Indonesia and Singapore, and founded several ventures of his own. His educational background is in accounting.

He runs two Estonian companies. At Nanas Sound OÜ ([nanas.ee](https://www.nanas.ee) and [nanas.build](https://www.nanas.build)) he builds his own products: music apps for iOS and macOS, DIY hardware, and an AI-assisted CAD and 3D printing service. Chloe & Matt OÜ ([chloematt.eu](https://chloematt.eu), reg. 14305217) is the company he uses for B2B software consulting and client invoicing.

Music is his personal craft. He plays piano and synthesizers and releases electronic and ambient music as Asep Bagja and bepitulaz. Many of the product ideas behind Nanas Sound start there. He began as a keyboardist in a college band in Indonesia around 2007 and played as a session keyboardist from 2007 to 2009 across alternative rock, pop, electronic pop, and jazz.
